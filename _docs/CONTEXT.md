# devrobotics.dev

Hardik Devrangadi's personal site: robotics portfolio, the Polaris assistant's public dashboard, and Frames, his photography.

## Language

### Frames (photography)

**Frames**:
The photography section of the site, reached from the top navigation.
_Avoid_: Gallery, Photography page; never call a single image "a frame"

**Photo**:
A single published image with its Note, Place and Shot details.
_Avoid_: Picture, image, post, frame

**Story**:
A long-form entry about one place or experience: a cover Photo, several paragraphs, more Photos, and optionally clips and videos.
_Avoid_: Blog, post, article, album

**Tile**:
One square in the Frames grid, showing either a Photo or a Story's cover.
_Avoid_: Thumbnail, card

**Note**:
The thought Hardik had when he took a Photo, a sentence or two in his own words.
_Avoid_: Caption, description

**Place**:
Where a Photo was taken, written by hand as a human-readable name (e.g. "Kyoto, Japan").
_Avoid_: Location, caption

**Shot details**:
The capture information shown for a Photo: camera, lens, focal length, aperture, shutter, ISO, dimensions, date taken and, when allowed, GPS coordinates.
_Avoid_: Metadata (that means the raw data embedded in a file), EXIF, specs

**Inbox**:
The private folder on the Pi where Hardik drops original Photos and Stories with their `.md` files, waiting to be published.
_Avoid_: Uploads, drafts folder
