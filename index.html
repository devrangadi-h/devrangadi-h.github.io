01 · About

From hexacopters to fulfilment-scale robots

I started in electronics and communication engineering in Bangalore, where I helped build The Eclipse, an autonomous hexacopter we flew at AUVSI SUAS 2019. Since then I’ve trained on-device vision models at Samsung R&D, shipped software at PwC and earned a master’s in Robotics at Northeastern. Today I deploy robotic systems at fulfilment-network scale at Amazon Robotics.

03 · Graduate research

An autonomous UAV for high-altitude atmospheric data

At Northeastern University, with Prof. Rifat Sipahi, I led the development of a UAV that collects atmospheric data at different altitudes.

  • SensingOnboard humidity, temperature, pressure, wind speed and wind direction sensors with Python drivers for real-time acquisition.
  • FlightDesigned the guidance, navigation and control system and validated it in planned flight tests that were both manual and autonomous.
